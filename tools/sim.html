⚔️ Fight simulator

Plays whole quest runs (5–10 fights and a boss) with the game's real fight rules, for a good player (picks the best card 90% of the time, 90% right answers), an average player (55%, 70%) and a guessing player (random cards, 25% right answers). It checks the goals of spec 03 §1. "Wiz/Math" is the wizard level and the math level. Fights are counted for normal monsters without abilities, except the boss and group columns.
